# Novelty Report

## Stress-Round Decision

Consensus from the bounded `hypothesis_scout`, `falsifier`, `novelty_checker`, and
`integrator` round:

- `H1_frontier_witness_certificate`: **pivot**
- `H2_prime_support_fixed_point`: **kill**
- strongest overlap risk: **Ford multiplication-table / divisor-in-an-interval**
- portfolio decision: **continue only one bounded H1 rescue/falsification experiment**

## H1 Decision: Pivot

The strong H1 claim does not survive the current corpus.

- `results/claims/h1_frontier_witness_certificate.md` shows that on record gaps
  `13`, `17`, `19`, `20`, and `21`, the only stable T-specific motif is the single
  axis-1 boundary marker `1 * previous_column_term`.
- The rest of each interval is covered by heterogeneous mostly-singleton witnesses:
  singleton shares are `10/12`, `14/16`, `15/18`, `17/19`, and `16/20`.
- `results/concept_evolve/probe_result.json` extends the same trend to larger record
  gaps: tiny-factor witnesses decay while balanced-factor witnesses grow.

Decision: keep H1 only as a narrowed rescue question about **full witness hypergraphs**
or higher-level invariants. Do not keep the raw-witness certificate story as an active
mechanism claim.

## H2 Decision: Kill

The backup line fails its own promotion test.

- `results/claims/h2_prime_support_fixed_point.md` shows that the prime-support
  observables add no explanatory power beyond the shared witness corpus.
- Composite-only record gaps already occur at `17`, `20`, and `21`, and
  `results/concept_evolve/probe_result.json` extends that to `28` and `30`.
- The row/column prime-support counts change too smoothly to explain when new records
  appear.

Decision: stop spending budget on H2. It remains a descriptive background state, not an
active explanatory line.

## Strongest Overlap Risk

The strongest overlap risk is still the Ford branch documented in
`results/literature/prior_art_gap.md`.

- The current witness evidence mostly says that skipped integers have some factor pair
  in the local border product set.
- Without a genuinely mex-coupled certificate or invariant, that reads as rebranded
  divisor/product coverage rather than new mathematics about this array.
- OEIS `A129258`, `A129259`, and Kimberling remain the provenance baseline; any
  positive-mechanism claim must clear both that baseline and the Ford overlap branch.

## Surviving Differentiated Contribution

What remains after rejecting the original H1 mechanism claim is narrower than the
original champion hypothesis:

- a validated negative result that the current raw-witness corpus does **not** compress
  into a compact T-specific certificate language;
- one thin endogenous boundary marker at the previous column term;
- an explicit overlap-control argument explaining why the package would collapse into a
  Ford-style restatement if overclaimed.

This is differentiated only as a careful **negative-result / overlap-audit dossier**
around Kimberling's open problem, not as a new asymptotic mechanism.

## Next-Step Gate

One further experiment is justified.

- Export full witness hypergraphs for late record gaps and test whether they admit a
  low-description-length invariant that size-matched surrogate product sets do not.
- If that rescue fails, kill H1 as well and conclude that the current hypothesis family
  does not support a novel positive mechanism claim.
