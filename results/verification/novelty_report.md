# Novelty Report

## Surviving Claim

The honest surviving claim is narrow:

- this run built an exact verifier-backed width-2 corridor search and benchmark pipeline,
- the frozen CA routes were falsified cleanly,
- and the only surviving exact objects are small direct corridor controls around score `2.0`.

This is **not**:

- a new arithmetic-Kakeya theorem,
- a new formulation of arithmetic Kakeya,
- evidence that forcing is a cellular automaton,
- or evidence of a reusable abelian-network or decoder mechanism.

  - the positive CA route failed, so the final artifact is a negative result against that analogy in this corridor family.
- Abelian-network literature:
  - the abelian layer added no screening value beyond raw arithmetic descriptors and was killed.
- Local-decoder folklore:
  - no reserve H3 route was activated, so no decoder-style claim survives.

## Evidence Direction

The evidence rejects the CA route for this corridor run.

- `H1_macrocell_substitution`: rejected by exact one-seed obstruction.
- `H2_target_direction_abelian`: rejected as a non-additive screening layer.
- Direct corridor residual: weakens any CA story further because the surviving witness is boundary-sensitive, non-robust, and not scale-stable.

## Open Risks And Assumptions

- The result is corridor-local only, not a statement about all constructible graphs.
- The direct-search frontier is heuristic, not exhaustive.
- The surviving controls are tiny finite-size objects and should not be over-interpreted.
- The final novelty language must stay operational and negative; any stronger positive wording would overrun the bibliography and the verified evidence.
