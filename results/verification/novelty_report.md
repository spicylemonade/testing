## Central sets generated by uniformly recurrent words

This paper is a real neighboring branch because it studies uniformly recurrent words, Sturmian structure, and central/IP sets with rich homogeneous-equation consequences. The overlap risk is category error: central sets and symbolic recurrence do not by themselves produce a homogeneous constant-coefficient recurrence for the ordered integer sequence `floor(n_k r)`. Our definition lock and H1 memo keep the project on the arithmetic side by demanding exact recurrences of numeric values under frozen selector classes.

Material difference: the project's theorem language is about exact recurrences of ordered Beatty values, not central sets generated by symbolic recurrence.

## Pisot numbers, Salem numbers, and generalised polynomials

This is the closest existing paper to the tempting higher-degree Pisot narrative. Byszewski-Konieczny show that some Pisot/Salem recurrence-value sets are generalized-polynomial definable, which makes them the natural exception branch for any irrational survivor. Our H2 backup explicitly tests that branch via frozen convergent constructions and finds that the broad Pisot-only story fails: quadratic Pisot slopes survive, cubic Pisot plastic does not, and the non-Pisot quadratic control `sqrt(2)` also survives.

Material difference: the current project studies ordered Beatty-subsequence embeddings and their exact recurrence certificates, not generalized-polynomial definability of standalone recurrence-value sets.

## Beatty Sequences for a Quadratic Irrational: Decidability and Applications

This is the key quadratic prior-art anchor. Schaeffer-Shallit-Zorcic prove synchronization and first-order decidability for quadratic Beatty relations in Ostrowski numeration. We use that paper as a boundary marker: any claim that only restates synchronization, definability, or additive-basis decidability is not novel here. The project's new content lies elsewhere: the H1 obstruction theorem for positive-density selectors and the H2 falsification of a broad Pisot-only extension.

Material difference: the contribution here is a recurrence-survivor classification and obstruction screen, not the decidability of the Beatty graph itself.

## Louisville Seamount Trail: implications for geodynamic mantle flow models and the geochemical evolution of primary hotspots

This watchlist entry is a lexical false positive and should not appear in the mathematical novelty discussion except as evidence that naive keyword search is unsafe. It has no shared objects, methods, or claims with Beatty sequences, linear recurrences, or numeration systems.

Material difference: the Louisville paper is a geology expedition report, whereas the current work is a number-theoretic and symbolic classification study.

## A Data Prefetch Mechanism for Accelerating General-Purpose Computation

This is another lexical false positive from the original search frontier. It offers no mathematical overlap with the Beatty subsequence problem and is useful only as a search-hygiene warning.

Material difference: the prefetch paper studies hardware and memory latency, while the current project studies exact recurrences inside Beatty value sequences.
