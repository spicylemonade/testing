# CA Framing Versus Search Prior

## Question

Did the cellular-automata framing contribute mathematical power, search-prior value, or mainly a re-encoding of the same direct arithmetic corridor search?

## Mathematical Gain

No mathematical gain survived verification.

- The frozen `H1_macrocell_substitution` route never produced a forceable exact certificate.
- The failure is not a weak empirical miss; it is an exact one-seed obstruction.
- The `H2_target_direction_abelian` backup also failed to add mathematical leverage, because its invariant package gave no screening advantage beyond raw support size, rank, and target-solvable counts.

Therefore the verified evidence does **not** support a claim that the CA framing improved arithmetic Kakeya certificates mathematically.

## Search-Prior Gain

There is a limited search-prior gain only.

- The corridor grammar and exact verifier pipeline did focus the search onto small legal witnesses quickly.
- Within that pipeline, direct no-CA corridor search found exact witnesses around score `2.0`.

But the ablations show that even this gain is weak:

- boundary-seed removal kills the best witness,
- `R` and `T` randomization kill it,
- fixed-`X` scaling fails,
- random `X` relabelings often preserve the same score.

So the surviving effect is closer to a boundary-heavy search bias than to a reusable CA mechanism.

## Reparameterization Risk

The evidence is more consistent with a re-encoding / search-bias story than with a new route.

- H1 reduced to a narrow interface encoding that never escaped the one-seed bottleneck.
- H2 reduced to a relabeling of support/rank information already visible in direct arithmetic features.
- The surviving witnesses stay in a tiny fixed-`X` corridor regime that matches the literature's bounded-slope / rational-complexity warning template.

## Surviving Claim

Any surviving claim is narrower than a successful “CA-guided certificate search” story.

The honest surviving claim is:

- an exact verifier-backed corridor research program was built,
- the frozen CA routes failed,
- and small direct corridor witnesses around score `2.0` remain as finite-size arithmetic controls only.
