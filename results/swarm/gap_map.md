# Gap Map

## Current Readout

- This file supersedes the earlier pre-verification gap map.
- Repo-local exact status:
  - `H1_macrocell_substitution` is killed by an exact one-seed obstruction before any score comparison.
  - `H2_target_direction_abelian` is killed because it adds no screening lift over raw arithmetic descriptors on the tested family IDs.
  - The best surviving exact objects are direct width-2 corridor controls around score `2.0`, and the saved ablations show boundary sensitivity, frozen-`X` non-transfer, and fragility under `R/T` perturbation.
- Consequence: the negative space is no longer “find another CA metaphor.” It is the set of under-served certificate regimes, interface tests, and exact controls that remain once the broad CA story has already failed.
- Not primary gaps:
  - generic bootstrap-percolation reformulations,
  - abelian-network rebranding,
  - local-decoder / Tanner analogies.
  These are overlap traps or negative-control lanes unless they beat matched arithmetic baselines on exact extracted score.

## Priority Gaps

### 1. Certificate-carrying interface bandwidth

- Under-served signal:
  - The frozen H1 macrocell route dies before scoring, and the concept-evolution layer promotes `bandwidth_first_rejection_test` and typed residue interfaces as the only live CA-positive rescue.
  - No saved artifact freezes a finite interface alphabet and then shows that it carries enough residue state for exact level-2 composition under one unchanged extractor.
- Why it matters:
  - If interfaces carry only occupancy or seed counts, level-2 composition needs hidden global repair and the CA layer collapses to search prior only.
  - This is the cleanest place where “cellular automata” could still mean something non-cosmetic.
- Concrete falsifier:
  - Freeze one typed residue interface alphabet.
  - Count canonical interface traces versus exact residue-summary classes from level-1 extraction.
  - Require identical extracted families when rollout is replaced by exhaustive interface composition.
  - Kill the route if the trace language becomes periodic or constant-size before level-2 improves exact score over matched no-CA search.
- Evidence:
  - `results/phase4_h1_frontier.md`
  - `results/concept_evolve/probe_result.json`
  - `results/concept_evolve/bridge_candidates.json`
  - `results/concept_evolve/concept_delta.md`

### 2. Mesoscopic low-height asymmetric `X` and slowly growing `X`

- Under-served signal:
  - The saved literature notes that the explicit `1.6751308` frontier is reached only as `X` grows without bound.
  - The in-repo exact survivors stay in a tiny fixed-`X` same-sum corridor basin around score `2.0`.
  - The pre-registered non-CA baselines `baseline_low_height_asymmetric_X`, `baseline_bounded_slope`, and `baseline_slowly_growing_X` are still missing.
- Why it matters:
  - This is the arithmetic regime most likely to separate a genuinely reusable local grammar from both trivial tiny-`X` retuning and huge-`X` asymptotics.
  - If no bounded or slowly growing low-height `X` family can beat the current basin, the CA framing loses its most plausible ownership claim.
- Concrete falsifier:
  - Freeze a benchmark ladder over `|X|`, coordinate height, determinant pattern, and asymmetry.
  - Run matched exact frontiers for fixed tiny `X`, low-height asymmetric `X`, bounded-slope controls, and slowly growing `X`.
  - Kill the regime if improvements only appear through exploding coordinate height or silently leaving the matched budget.
- Evidence:
  - `results/literature/prior_art_gap.md`
  - `results/final_handoff.md`
  - `results/phase2_baseline_matrix.md`
  - `results/verification/benchmark_report.md`

### 3. Geometry beyond the frozen width-2 corridor

- Under-served signal:
  - Every exact positive row in the repo is a height-2 corridor control; the pre-registered `H in {2,3,4}` benchmark ladder was never executed beyond `H=2`.
  - Stage-order and aspect-ratio perturbations remain mostly unexecuted, so the current failures may still be geometry-specific rather than structural.
- Why it matters:
  - A CA-like local mechanism, if it exists, should survive beyond one thin corridor encoding.
  - Height-3 and height-4 corridors, transposed families, and phase-coded or aperiodic cross-sections are under-tested but still certificate-first.
- Concrete falsifier:
  - Run matched `H=3` and `H=4` corridors, plus transposed and stage-order-perturbed variants, under the same `X`, grammar-size, and search budgets.
  - Require any gain to survive aspect-ratio and stage-order perturbations without new extractor logic.
  - Kill the setting if all wins collapse back to the width-2 boundary program.
- Evidence:

### 4. Seed-minimal defect transport versus boundary programming

- Under-served signal:
  - The best exact witnesses are boundary-heavy and fragile: boundary-seed removal and `R/T` randomization kill them, while `X` relabelings often do not.
  - The one-seed H1 obstruction and the ablation record together suggest that seed placement, not bulk dynamics, is carrying the surviving controls.
- Why it matters:
  - The real negative space is not another homogeneous automaton. It is whether sparse defects, sinks, and asymmetric seed budgets can move anti-diagonal residue efficiently enough to lower `r` without buying the score through boundary initialization.
  - This is a concrete CA-like setting that remains under-built rather than over-discussed.
- Concrete falsifier:
  - Benchmark a fixed defect-budget axis: corner-defect starts, typed sinks, and minimal boundary seed schedules on nearly regular products.
  - Require gains to survive boundary-seed thinning, `R/T` randomization, and orientation perturbations.
  - Kill the route if score gains disappear as soon as the seed budget is normalized.
- Evidence:
  - `results/phase4_ablations.md`
  - `results/phase5_ca_vs_search_prior.md`
  - `results/phase5_negative_results.md`
  - `results/literature/prior_art_gap.md`

### 5. Benchmark-complete exact controls and complexity accounting

- Under-served signal:
  - The benchmark report still flags a missing matched `2 x 8` same-palette control, missing non-CA baselines, confounded scale tests, thin trace logging, and absent symmetry-quotient registry.
  - The saved tool plan is pre-falsification unless explicitly superseded.
- Why it matters:
  - Without a frozen family registry, symmetry quotient, and hidden-complexity ledger, CA claims remain unfalsifiable: score can improve by hiding cost in `X`, coordinate height, state alphabet, extractor logic, or scale-specific macrotiles.
  - In this problem, benchmark infrastructure is not clerical. It is part of the mathematical negative space because it determines whether any claimed CA signal is real.
- Concrete falsifier:
  - Freeze named family IDs, symmetry quotienting, and matched baselines before reopening any bridge.
  - Log per-trial verifier success or failure, score, failure reason, `seed_budget`, `initial_t_budget`, `|X|`, coordinate height, active-state count, and grammar length.
  - Treat any route that needs looser logging or unmatched controls as non-evidence.

## Why These Five

- They stay inside the exact finite-certificate setting already validated in the repo.
- They target under-served regimes or failure surfaces that survived the H1 and H2 kills.
- They are cheap enough to falsify without another broad literature or compute-heavy expansion.
- They do not depend on marketing generic CA, abelian, or decoder language as a contribution.

## Routing Implication

- Do not reopen `H1` or `H2` as named positive routes.
- If work continues, the only live positive lane is:
  - typed residue interfaces with bandwidth-first rejection,
  - on low-height asymmetric `X`,
  - under matched no-CA baselines,
  - with boundary-sensitivity and hidden-complexity screens mandatory.
- Otherwise, the honest contribution remains a negative benchmark-and-falsification result.
