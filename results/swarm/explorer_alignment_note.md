## 3. Differentiation Notes for Watchlist Papers and the 2025 Frontier Paper
- **Cellular Automata Applications in Shortest Path Problem (2017):** generic CA optimization or survey material for pathfinding, networks, and biomimetic routing; not Hadamard search, not exact orthogonality, and not seed-matched to the order-`668` frontier object.
- **Learning Automata-Based Solutions to the Single Elevator Problem (2019):** learning-automata control for elevator parking, not cellular automata, not combinatorial-design search, and not exact feasibility over dense Gram constraints.
- **On the possibility of oscillating in the Ebola virus dynamics and investigating the effect of the lifetime of T lymphocytes (2022):** biomedical dynamics paper with no meaningful technical overlap beyond noisy lexical matches on automata-adjacent terms.
- **Engineering Societies in the Agents World (2000):** agent-systems or social-coordination material, not a CA repair paper and not a matrix-search paper; treat it as retrieval noise, not real prior-art pressure.
- **Learning Automata-Based Solutions to the Multi-Elevator Problem (2019):** same separation as the single-elevator paper; LA-based control or resource allocation, not CA, not Hadamard, and not exact search over orthogonality constraints.
- **A 64-Modular Hadamard Matrix of Order 668 (2025):** this is not a competing optimizer but the frontier anchor; it supplies the compact `q/s` seed, the `13` exceptional correlations, and the defect profile, so any defensible contribution here starts after this paper as seeded repair or search on top of its near-solution.
