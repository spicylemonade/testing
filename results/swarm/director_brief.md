# Director Brief

## Decision

- Champion: `H1_macrocell_substitution`
- Backup: `H2_target_direction_abelian`
- Reserve only: `H3_slope_bloom`

## Why H1 Leads

- It has the best novelty-to-overlap balance in the current artifact set. The main gap is not “find another local metaphor,” but “find a certificate-first recursive grammar whose interfaces compose and improve score across scale.”
- It lines up with the strongest repo-local openings: anisotropic recursive products, defect or boundary structure, and the compile-to-certificate bottleneck.
- Its falsifier is clean and cheap. A level-1 versus level-2 transfer check immediately tells us whether recursion is real or whether interface overhead simply recreates tensor-product costs.

## Why H2 Is the Backup

- It is the cleanest semantic fit to forcing rule `3`, so it remains the best fallback once H1 is tested.
- It is not the champion because the repackaging risk is higher. The honest claim is narrow: abelian invariants as a screening device for exact certificates, not “arithmetic Kakeya forcing is an abelian network.”
- It becomes active only if H1 fails on interface composition or transfer.

## Why H3 Stays Reserve

- It has the highest novelty upside, but it also has the fastest failure path back into the bounded-slope or rational-complexity basin.
- It is less directly aligned with exact verifier-compatible certificates than H1 or H2.
- Keep it only as a hedge after the first two routes have a frozen grammar and baseline matrix.

## Deprioritized Branches

- Critical-droplet anisotropic forcing: too easy to collapse into bootstrap-percolation-plus-compiler.
- Integer-lifted Tanner gadgets with local decoders: too easy to collapse into local-decoder-plus-compiler.

## Hard Blocker

No route should advance past planning until three items are frozen:

1. one exact certificate grammar for `(X; d_i; f_i; T; R)`,
2. one matched baseline matrix,
3. one symmetry-quotient policy.

Without those, complexity can be hidden in the extractor, in `X`, or in scale-specific macrotiles, and the CA framing becomes untestable.

## Exact Next Experiment For The Researcher

Do not run a broad search. Instead:

1. Fix one finite macro-cell interface grammar for `H1`.
2. Compile level-1 and level-2 instances into exact legal `(X; d_i; f_i; T; R)` certificates on low-height asymmetric `X`.
3. Compare the extracted score frontier against a direct no-CA search on the same geometry.

Pivot immediately to `H2` if either of these happens:

- level-2 transfer is flat or worse than level-1,
- exact extraction requires bespoke global repair not present in the grammar.
