# Director Brief

## Decision

Champion: `H_comp_packet_graph_ca_668`.

This is the best remaining novelty-to-falsifiability tradeoff after the repo's executed `H1` failure. It does not recycle the dead single-packet q/s lattice. Instead it attacks the actual blocker the scouts identified: fake locality. The branch survives only if a retained low-splash composite packet library exists and a graph CA on that library still beats CA-off or scorer-only controls in the same basis. That makes it both genuinely different and easy to kill quickly.

Backup: `H_lag_residue_ca_167`.

This is the strongest representation pivot if packet-space locality stays fake even after actuator redesign. Its advantage is real representation-level novelty. Its risk is equally clear: it can collapse into Williamson, Turyn, Goethals-Seidel, cocyclic, or block-circulant search, so it must stay behind a hard family-leakage audit from the first run.

Reserve: `H_decoder_relay_ca_frontier`.

Keep this only if the project is willing to accept a hybrid claim. It is verifiable, but the exact layer can easily absorb the novelty credit.

## Why H1 Is Out

Do not spend new budget on `H1_defect_syndrome_ca_64m`. The scout pack is aligned on this point: the current one-packet basis is fake-local on the canonical seed, exhaustive one-packet and two-packet scans expose no improving move, and the saved H1 runs never beat the seed objective `13 / 2880 / 512`. `H1` now serves as the negative control, not as an active lead.

## Current Blocker

The remaining blocker is not missing literature. It is missing mechanism isolation. The repo still lacks the decisive artifact that separates "CA failed" from "the actuator basis and locality geometry were wrong":

- a retained low-splash alternative packet library, and
- CA-off or scorer-only ablations on the same retained basis.

Without those, reopening CA execution would just repeat the failed H1 framing.

## Exact Next Experiment For The Researcher

1. Freeze `H1_defect_syndrome_ca_64m` as the negative control and add CA-off plus scorer-only ablations in the same harness.
2. Enumerate symmetry-safe `2`-packet, `4`-packet, and run-boundary composites around the canonical seed; retain only moves with sharply lower changed-lag footprint and at least improving or strategically neutral frontier behavior.
3. Build the sparse defect-packet influence graph from the retained library.
4. Run one matched kill test on the canonical seed and one harder control: graph CA or HCA stress redistribution versus CA-off, scorer-only or weighted bit-flip, `greedy`, `tabu`, and `simulated_annealing`.
5. Stop immediately if basis sharing removes the gain, whole-basis rescoring remains necessary, or exact-hit rate stays flat.

If that kill test fails because packet-space locality remains fake even after the retained library, open `H_lag_residue_ca_167` next with the family-leakage audit enabled from the first run.
