## 6. Decentralized Interleaving of Parallel-connected Buck Converters (2019)
- Why it is close: This line of work already uses decentralized coupled oscillators to control phase relationships among parallel buck cells, which substantially overlaps any generic "firefly" or "quorum-sensing" pitch for multiphase converter synchronization.
- Differentiation hypothesis: The only version that may still survive is an explicitly spectral controller that keeps the converter near cluster synchrony on purpose to flatten EMI peaks under changing loads, rather than simply achieving stable interleaving.
- Evidence artifact(s): "Decentralized Interleaving of Parallel-connected Buck Converters"; "A Distributed Phase Management Scheme for Interleaved Buck Converters".
- Pivot decision (if any): Pivoted away from oscillator-based interleaving as a headline bridge hypothesis because the novelty margin looked weak relative to existing coupled-oscillator converter control.
