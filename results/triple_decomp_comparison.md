# Triple Decomposition Search Results

## Method
Pythagorean triple (m,n) parametrization with leg-based indexing.
Generates all primitive triples and multiples, indexes by leg values,
finds pairs sharing a common leg, checks third face diagonal.

## Results
- Search bound: 10,000
- Candidate pairs tested: 323,618
- Euler bricks found: 151
- Perfect cuboids found: 0
- Wall clock time: 0.19s
- Throughput: 1,730,352 pairs/sec

## Comparison with Baseline
The triple decomposition approach is orders of magnitude more efficient
than brute-force because it only examines (a,b,c) triples where at least
two face diagonals are already guaranteed to be integers.
