### Key Findings

1. **Current state-of-the-art**: Muła/Lemire (2020) achieved near-memcpy speed with AVX-512 VBMI (~0.2 cycles/byte). The PSHUFB-bitmask lookup (0.75 instr/char) + multiply-add packing (2 instructions) form the established optimal pipeline.

2. **Unexplored territory**: GFNI (VGF2P8AFFINEQB) has not been applied to Base64 decode despite being available since Ice Lake (2019). It could reduce the lookup phase from 12 to 4-6 instructions.

3. **ARM gap**: SVE/SVE2 Base64 decoders are essentially uncharted — existing ARM implementations are NEON-only (128-bit). SVE on Graviton3 (256-bit) could double ARM throughput.

4. **Fusion opportunity**: Fusing decode with CRC/HMAC verification (common in JWT, S/MIME) eliminates a full memory pass, potentially the largest real-world performance gain for applications that both decode and verify.
