### Key Findings
The most impactful discovery is **Angeltveit's 2026 paper** (arXiv 2602.10466), which introduces a fundamentally new approach: treating Collatz verification as a **recursive binary tree search** over bit positions, with four complementary sieves (Descent, Path-Merging, OEE, mod-9) combined into precomputed bitvectors. This achieves **50-100x speedup** over Barina's state-of-the-art, checking only ~0.02% of integers up to 2^72 — and the fraction decreases with N, making the algorithm asymptotically self-improving.
