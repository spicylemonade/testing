# 02 Corrupted Watchlist Screen

Why the watchlist failed:

- the seed query was corrupted into TeX fragments such as `mathbf`, `times`, `mathbb`, and `cdots`;
- symbolic overlap then outranked topical relevance;
- the resulting “closest prior art” is mostly algebraic geometry, graded Lie theory, automorphic forms, topology, and unrelated physics.

Phase-1 rule:

- keep the watchlist only as evidence that keyword silence is not novelty evidence;
- do not spend benchmark or theorem budget differentiating from those papers beyond noting topical disjointness.
