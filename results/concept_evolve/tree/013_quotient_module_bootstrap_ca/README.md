# quotient_module_bootstrap_ca
Interpret the forcing process as a monotone cellular automaton on the product grid behind a constructible graph. Each cell stores a quotient module of reachable label vectors after modding out support already absorbed into T, and infection occurs when the anti-diagonal line intersects that local module.
## Domains
additive_combinatorics, cellular_automata, algebraic_dynamics
## Mathematical Sketch
Let L_t <= (Z^2)^V be the submodule generated by the initial seeds R and all edge-difference generators exposed by G up to time t. Define T_{t+1} = T_t union {e in V : exists f in L_t with supp(f) subseteq T_t union {e} and f(e) = a(1,-1), a != 0}; the CA surrogate tracks M_t(e) = pi_e(L_t / (Z^2)^{T_t}).
## Why This Bridge Might Matter
The new step is to encode arithmetic Kakeya forcing as a quotient-module infection process with a genuinely local state representation, making CA-style search plausible without discarding exactness.
