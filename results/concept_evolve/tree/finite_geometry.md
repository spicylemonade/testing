# Finite Geometry and Block Designs

## Concept
Using algebraic curves over finite fields (e.g., $\mathbb{F}_q$) to generate graphs with specific intersection properties. Highly symmetric graphs (like strongly regular graphs) often provide the best lower bounds for Ramsey numbers.

## Analogical Connection
The Paley graph is a classic example of using quadratic residues in finite fields to avoid large cliques. For $R(5,5)$, we can explore Hermitian curves, Suzuki curves, or more general block designs where adjacency is defined by incidence in a finite projective plane.
