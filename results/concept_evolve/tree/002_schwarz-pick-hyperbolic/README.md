# Schwarz-Pick Hyperbolic

The Schwarz-Pick lemma states that holomorphic self-maps of the disk are contractions in the hyperbolic metric. In the univalent setting, this provides lower bounds on the hyperbolic density of the image domain, which translates directly to inradius estimates. The hyperbolic perspective offers a coordinate-free approach to distortion that connects naturally to covering surface theory and extremal length.
