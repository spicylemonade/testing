# Analysis

This branch is experimentally invalidated for the promoted close-encounter wedge.

- Across `dt = 0.04, 0.02, 0.01, 0.005`, the resolution-coupled softening mode never enters the declared safe regime.
- It reduces neither final-state error nor energy drift enough to compete with the encounter-microstep branch.

Result: keep the idea as an honesty warning for future visualization modes, but do not use it as a fidelity-preserving fix.
